Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UGC Poster ("the app"), operated by Isaiah Choy ("we"), handles information. UGC Poster is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not available to the public.

1. Information the app accesses

When the owner connects one of their own accounts, the platform shares, with the owner's permission:

  • Account identifiers: account ID, username or channel name, used to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Access tokens: credentials issued by the platform that allow the app to upload videos to that account.
  • Post results: the ID and link of each video the app publishes, and whether the upload succeeded.

The app requests only the permissions needed to upload and publish videos (for example YouTube's youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes, Instagram's content publishing permission, and TikTok's video.publish/video.upload and basic profile scopes). It does not access messages, comments, followers, contacts, analytics of other users or any other personal data.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to publish the owner's scheduled videos to the owner's own accounts and to show the owner whether each post succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder that only the owner's user account can read. They are not uploaded to any server operated by us. Data is sent only to the platforms themselves (Meta, Google/YouTube, TikTok) over encrypted HTTPS connections, as part of their official APIs.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any information to third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform the owner chose to publish them on.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only while an account is connected. Disconnecting an account deletes its token from the owner's computer. Access can also be revoked at any time from each platform:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account → Third-party connections
  • Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites (or Business Integrations)
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

6. YouTube and Google

UGC Poster uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. UGC Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

UGC Poster is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
